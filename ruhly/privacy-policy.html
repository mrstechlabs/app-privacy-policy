Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 2026

Your privacy is important to us. Ruhly is designed to work entirely on your device — we do not collect, store, or share your personal data.
📋

Information We Collect

Ruhly does not collect any personally identifiable information. All data you enter in the app — prayer logs, habits, streaks — is stored locally on your device only.

We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.

100% Offline & Private

All your habit and prayer data lives on your device. We never see it, never store it on servers, never sell it.

📍

Location Data

Ruhly requests your location solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your area using the Adhan.js library. This is processed entirely on-device.

Your location is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

🔔

Notifications

If you enable prayer time notifications (Ruhly Pro feature), all notification scheduling happens locally on your device using your calculated prayer times. No data leaves your phone.

💳

In-App Purchases

Ruhly offers a one-time Pro upgrade processed through Google Play. We do not handle or store any payment information. All transactions are managed by Google Play's secure payment system.

We use RevenueCat to manage and verify purchase status. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase data as described in their privacy policy.

👶

Children's Privacy

Ruhly does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

✏️

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

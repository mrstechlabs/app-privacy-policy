Privacy Policy

Effective June 14, 2026

The short version

TodoX stores your tasks on your device. We don't collect your data, we don't track you, and we don't run ads. That's it.

What we collect

Nothing. TodoX is offline-first with no account, no signup, and no analytics SDKs. Your tasks live in a local database on your phone and never leave it unless you choose to enable Backup & Sync (a Pro feature).

Purchases

TodoX Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not handle or store any payment information. Google's own privacy terms govern that transaction.

Third-party services

TodoX does not include advertising networks, analytics frameworks, crash reporters, or any third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Children's privacy

TodoX does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to identify a child or any other user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because we don't collect contact information, we can't notify you directly — check back if you'd like.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@todox.app.
